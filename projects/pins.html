Overview

Pins is an interactive world map where anyone can place a pin, attach a description, and share meaningful places with others. It turns geography into a canvas for personal and collective storytelling.

The Problem

Maps are powerful tools for navigation, but they rarely capture the emotional or narrative weight of a place. There was no simple way for people to mark and share the spots that matter to them in a public, exploratory space.

The Solution

A shared map experience that invites contribution. Users drop pins, write short stories or descriptions, and explore what others have left behind — creating a living archive of places and the meaning attached to them.

Key Features

  • Interactive global map with place-based pins
  • User-generated descriptions and stories
  • Exploration-first interface designed for discovery
  • Community-driven content that grows over time

Technologies

JavaScript Map APIs HTML / CSS User-generated content

Challenges

Designing an interface that feels inviting to first-time contributors while remaining clear for exploration. Handling geographic data thoughtfully and keeping the experience lightweight across devices.

Focus Areas

  • Community
  • Exploration
  • User-generated content
  • Geographic storytelling
