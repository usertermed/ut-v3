Just The News

A Lightweight News Reader

Visit Live Site ↗

Overview

Just The News is a minimalist news platform built to strip away recommendation algorithms, endless feeds, and visual noise. The goal: let readers browse current events in a clean, chronological experience that prioritizes the writing itself.

The Problem

Modern news apps are often optimized for engagement rather than understanding. Algorithmic feeds, autoplay video, and dense UI patterns compete for attention and make it harder to simply read the news.

The Solution

A focused reading experience with a clear editorial hierarchy, fast load times, and zero distraction layers. Content is presented chronologically. The interface stays out of the way so the stories can take center stage.

Key Features

  • Clean chronological feed without algorithmic ranking
  • Typography and spacing optimized for long-form readability
  • Fast performance and minimal client-side overhead
  • Editorial design language inspired by classic newspapers

Technologies

Next.js React TypeScript Tailwind CSS Vercel

Challenges

Balancing simplicity with enough structure to feel intentional. Designing a layout that feels premium and calm without becoming sparse or incomplete. Ensuring the experience remains fast even as content volume grows.

Focus Areas

  • Readability
  • Simplicity
  • Fast performance
  • Clean editorial design
